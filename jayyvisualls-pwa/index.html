✅
One-time setup takes about 10 minutes. After that, generating and tracking links per post takes under 2 minutes each.
Step-by-Step Setup
1
Create a free Bitly account
Go to bitly.com and sign up for free. The free plan gives you enough short links for your workflow. Bitly tracks every click automatically — no client access needed.
→ bitly.com → Sign Up Free
2
Add your clients in this system
Go to the Clients tab → Add each client with their name, Instagram handle, website URL, and niche. Atlas at Richland Road is already pre-loaded as your first client.
3
Before every post: generate a tracking link
Go to Link Generator → select the client → fill in the platform, content type, and post title → generate → copy the UTM URL → shorten on Bitly → save it here.
UTM URL → bitly.com/create → paste into post
4
Update click counts from Bitly (2x/week)
Log into Bitly → check your links dashboard → come to All Links tab → click the update button on each link → enter the current click count. Takes 2 minutes. Your dashboard updates automatically.
5
Show clients the traffic report
Open the Dashboard tab and screenshot it. This is your proof: "Your last 5 Reels generated X clicks to your website." That's your ROI story. No client backend access required.
6
(Optional) Upgrade: Ask clients for Google Analytics view access
If a client is open to it, ask them to add you as a Viewer in Google Analytics 4. Then you can see what people did after clicking — how long they stayed, what pages they visited, and if they filled out a form. Massive upgrade for proving ROI.
GA4 → Admin → Account Access Management → Add User (your email) → Viewer role
