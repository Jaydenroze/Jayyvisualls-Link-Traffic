STEP 1 — Deploy the app (free, 2 minutes)
──────────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to:  https://app.netlify.com/drop
   (no account needed for a quick test link)

2. Drag the entire "jayyvisualls-pwa" folder
   onto the Netlify page

3. Netlify gives you a URL like:
   https://random-name-123.netlify.app

   → For a permanent link, sign up free at netlify.com
     and claim the site. You can rename the URL too, e.g.:
     https://jayyvisualls.netlify.app


STEP 2 — Install on iPhone
────────────────────────────
1. Open the URL on your iPhone in Safari
   (must be Safari — Chrome won't work for install)

2. Tap the Share button  (box with arrow pointing up)

3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"

4. Name it "JayyVisualls" → tap Add

5. Done — it's on your home screen like a real app.
   Full screen, no browser bar, works offline.


NOTES:
──────
• Your data syncs between Mac and iPhone via the
  same Netlify URL — log in from any device.

• If you want your data private and not on a public
  URL, let me know — I can add a password screen.

• To update the app later, just drag a new version
  of the folder to Netlify Drop again.
